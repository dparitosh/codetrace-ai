## Domain reference checks

The configuration-identity analysis follows the OSLC distinction between concept resources and their configuration-specific states: [OSLC Configuration Management](https://docs.oasis-open-projects.org/oslc-op/config/v1.0/oslc-config-mgt.html#concept-resources-version-resources-and-configuration-context). The CycloneDX compatibility case follows the [official 1.6 JSON schema](https://github.com/CycloneDX/specification/blob/master/schema/bom-1.6.schema.json): component `type` and `name` are required, while `bom-ref` is optional. General cross-domain review used [OSLC Core](https://docs.oasis-open-projects.org/oslc-op/core/v3.0/oslc-core.html) and the [TRS specification](https://docs.oasis-open-projects.org/oslc-op/trs/v3.0/tracked-resource-set.html). These references support domain expectations; the observed behavior is established by the local fixtures and cited code.
